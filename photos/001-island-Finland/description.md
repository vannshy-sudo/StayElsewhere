# Privaatti saari / Private island
Uusikaupunki, Finland

## DESCRIPTION
3 cabins sleep up to 10 guests across 3 bedrooms with 4 beds and 1 bath. Solar-powered main cabin has a compact kitchen with hand-washing; wood-heated sauna with 120 kg Harvia Legend kiuas, rowboat, 2 piers, BBQ, and sandy beach access. No running water (rainwater and sea water used for washing); Wifi available in the main cabin.

## WHY I CHOSE THIS PLACE — DRAFT (review and rewrite)
Three cabins on a private island in the Finnish archipelago, reached by boat with no road access and no neighbours. No running water — rainwater and seawater only, solar power for what little electricity there is. The Baltic archipelago around Uusikaupunki is the reason this works: enough uninhabited islands nearby to row to, enough open water to make the isolation feel earned.
