# Privaatti saari / Private island
Uusikaupunki, Finland

## DESCRIPTION
3 small cabins with 3 bedrooms and 4 beds sleeping up to 10 guests, 1 bath. Solar-powered compact kitchen in main cabin with hand-washing only; no running water, heating by wood-burning stoves; rainwater and sea water used for washing. Private wood sauna, rowing boat, beach access, and BBQ included; wifi available.
