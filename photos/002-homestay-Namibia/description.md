# The Desert Light Shack
Swakopmund, Namibia

## DESCRIPTION
1 bedroom with 1 queen bed plus a living room with 1 queen bed and 1 couch, sleeping up to 2 guests, with 1 bath. Fully self-catering kitchen with french press coffee. Patio, backyard, free parking, and pets allowed; wifi status not listed.

## WHY I CHOSE THIS PLACE — DRAFT (review and rewrite)
Off-grid farm shack on the edge of the Namib, 30 minutes outside Swakopmund with no listed wifi and full self-catering only. The Namib at this latitude is one of the oldest deserts on earth, and the light and sky at night here are the reason to come. It is here because there is almost nowhere else in the region where you sleep this close to the desert floor without a lodge infrastructure around you.
